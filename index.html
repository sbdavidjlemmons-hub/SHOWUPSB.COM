The Pipeline

Streamlining

The problem is simpler than it looks.

A great deal of grant and public money comes into this community through service providers — and too often, outside agencies are the ones deciding how our money gets spent here. Nobody is looking at the whole board.

Providers step on each other

Two organizations, one block apart, funded to do the same thing, not talking.

Efforts get duplicated

The same intake, the same forms, the same waitlist — three times over, while the gap in between goes unfilled.

Trust falls through the gaps

The people who need help stop believing anyone will show up. That is exactly why trust in service providers is so low right now.

It takes coordination, transparency, and a little honest work. Resources move away from those taking advantage of the gift they were given — and toward the people showing real skill, real compassion, and real desire.
